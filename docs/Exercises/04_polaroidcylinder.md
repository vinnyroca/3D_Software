1. Create a cylinder with 12 side > Remove top and bottom ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_01.gif)
2. Rotate cylinder to match inner corner > Delete sides except 3 in corner ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_02.gif)
3. Use Mirror tool to create another corner > move over in face mode![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_03.gif)
4. Duplicate and rotate corner 90 degrees > Combine ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_04.gif)
5. Bridge faces together (Use **G** Hotkey to repeat last action) ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_05.gif)
6. Move Corners to correct positions in vertex mode ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_06.gif)
7. Duplicate object and move corners to match larger shape![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_07.gif)
8. Combine > Reverse the normals on the inner shape ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_08.gif)
9. Bridge shapes together, you may need to do only two edges first ![pShpere](/assets/images/4/04_pCyl_09.gif)
